About these terms

Welcome to Nexbeck. These Terms of Service set out the clear, mutual agreement between Nexbeck ("we", "us", "our") and our clients ("you", "your"). We operate from Pakistan, delivering custom web design and development services to electrical contractors and trade businesses across Australia.

These terms are written in plain Australian English to be fair, transparent, and balanced for both sides. They ensure you know exactly what to expect regarding pricing, milestone payments, turnaround times, revisions, and ownership.

Our services and packages

We provide custom, responsive website design, development, and launch services tailored specifically for trade businesses. We offer three primary packages:

  • Essential Package ($890 AUD, Under $1,000): Clean 3-page foundation (Home, Services, Contact) engineered for owner-operators needing a professional online presence. Includes 1 round of revisions and 7-day delivery.
  • Professional Package ($1,390 AUD, $1,000–$1,500): Complete 5-page setup (Home, Services, About, Projects/Gallery, Contact) with suburban service area landing pages and trade review integration. Includes 3 rounds of revisions and 7–14 day delivery.
  • Premium Package ($1,990 AUD, $1,500–$2,300): Multi-page authority platform with advanced quote builder or cost calculator, emergency electrical callout features, and multi-suburb coverage. Includes 5 rounds of revisions and 14–18 day delivery.

All packages include mobile optimisation, Google Analytics setup, fast Cloudflare hosting readiness, contact form integration, and baseline on-page SEO.

Quotes and acceptance

Before any project begins, we provide a clear written quote outlining the selected package, exact deliverables, fixed price in AUD (including GST), and estimated delivery timeline.

A project is accepted when you confirm the quote in writing (by email or electronic message) and pay the initial project milestone. Once accepted, we never change the agreed project price or deliverables without your prior written consent.

Payments

All prices are fixed and quoted in Australian Dollars (AUD) and include GST. To ensure complete fairness and keep both parties aligned throughout the build, payments are divided into three clear milestones:

Milestone Amount When Due
Milestone 1 30% of total After the site plan, wireframe, and brief are agreed upon
Milestone 2 30% of total When the frontend design and interactive pages are fully built
Milestone 3 40% of total When you have approved the site and it is ready to go live

Invoices are payable within 7 business days of issue via electronic bank transfer or approved card payment. Work on the next phase commences upon receipt of the corresponding milestone payment.

What we need from you

To deliver your website on schedule, we need reasonable collaboration from you, including:

  • Your business logo, high-resolution photos of your past electrical work, and any supplier or trade brand logos you are authorized to display.
  • Your trade contractor licence number and Australian Business Number (ABN).
  • Accurate descriptions of your core services and primary geographic service areas.
  • Access to your existing domain name registrar or DNS manager (if you already own a domain).
  • Timely feedback on project milestones and revision requests.

Delivery timeline

Our standard delivery timeframes by package are:

  • Essential: 7 calendar days
  • Professional: 7 to 14 calendar days
  • Premium: 14 to 18 calendar days

The delivery clock begins on the business day following both: (a) receipt of your completed project brief with required assets, and (b) receipt of the first milestone payment. If you require extra time to review drafts or provide content, the project clock is simply paused until you respond—ensuring you are never rushed.

Revisions

Every package includes structured revision rounds to ensure your website looks and functions exactly as you want:

  • Essential Package: 1 structured revision round
  • Professional Package: 3 structured revision rounds
  • Premium Package: 5 structured revision rounds

A revision round consists of a consolidated list of requested text edits, image swaps, layout tweaks, or colour adjustments submitted together. Revisions allow us to refine agreed deliverables. Requests for completely new features or extra pages outside the initial scope will be quoted separately before any work is undertaken.

Ownership and handover

You own your website. Once the final milestone payment (Milestone 3) is completed:

  • You own 100% of the custom website code, HTML/CSS/JavaScript files, graphics, and written content created for your project.
  • We hand over all source files, login credentials, and deployment keys directly to you.
  • If we assist in registering a new domain name on your behalf, you are registered as the sole legal registrant and owner of that domain.
  • We do not lock your site into proprietary platforms or hold your code hostage. You are free to host, modify, or transfer your website wherever you choose.

We reserve the right to display the completed website in our portfolio and marketing materials as an example of our work, unless you request otherwise in writing.

Domain and hosting add-on

For clients who prefer a completely hands-off setup, we offer an optional domain registration and high-performance hosting add-on for $149 AUD per year.

  • This includes an Australian domain registration (.com.au or .com), SSL security certification, DNS setup, and global Cloudflare edge hosting.
  • This add-on renews annually. We will send you an email reminder at least 30 days before any scheduled renewal date.
  • There are no lock-in contracts or exit penalties. You can cancel at any time before the renewal date with a simple email. If you cancel, we will transfer your domain and website files to your chosen provider at no extra charge.

Licence numbers and trade claims

When building your website, we display the business details, licence numbers, accreditations, and trade claims (e.g. "licensed & insured", Master Electricians membership, CEC solar accreditation) that you provide to us.

You are solely responsible for ensuring that all trade licence numbers, business names, ABNs, and regulatory statements provided are valid, accurate, and current. You are also responsible for verifying that your marketing claims satisfy your relevant state or territory electrical licensing regulator (such as Energy Safe Victoria, Fair Trading NSW, Electrical Safety Office QLD, Consumer and Business Services SA, or Building Commission WA).

SEO

Every website we build includes clean semantic HTML, fast loading speeds, clean URL structures, mobile responsiveness, Schema.org trade structured data, OpenGraph social cards, and XML sitemaps. These foundational technical elements give your site the best possible opportunity to be crawled and ranked by search engines.

However, search engine ranking algorithms (such as Google's) are independent, constantly evolving, and influenced by competitor activity, local market density, and third-party citations. Therefore, we do not guarantee specific numerical search rankings or page-one placements, and any agency that does is misleading you.

Free website audit

We provide a free website audit for trade businesses upon request. This audit is a practical, honest technical and visual review of your existing website (or competitor baseline) covering speed, mobile usability, contact clarity, and search crawlability.

The audit is 100% free with zero obligation to hire us. The findings and recommendations are provided for your information only, without warranties of any kind.

Cancellation and refunds

We stand behind our work and believe in fair, straightforward commercial relationships:

  • Refund if nothing is built: If you accept a quote and pay Milestone 1, but cancel before we have commenced wireframing or design work, you are entitled to a full refund of your payment.
  • Cancellation during development: Either party may terminate a project at any stage by providing written notice. In such an event, you will only be charged for milestones that have been completed. Any unspent portion of milestone payments for unstarted stages will be promptly refunded.
  • Work completed to date: If a project is cancelled partway through, you will receive all completed source files and assets corresponding to the milestones paid for.

Support after launch

We want your launch to be completely seamless. Every website package includes 30 days of post-launch warranty support at no extra cost.

During this 30-day window, we will promptly fix any bugs, display errors, broken links, or contact form delivery issues that arise. For ongoing content updates, new pages, or design additions beyond the 30-day window, we offer hourly support or simple fixed-price update quotes.
