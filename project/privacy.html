DATA TRANSPARENCY & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026
Plain-English Summary
  • We only collect details you provide to answer your enquiry, prepare your website proposal, or complete your project.
  • We store contact form enquiry data for 30 days before deleting it from our form records.
  • We never sell your personal information or share it with third-party advertisers.
  • We operate from Pakistan while building websites for Australian trade businesses, using secure tools like Cloudflare, Zoho Mail, and Web3Forms.
  • You can ask us to view, update, or permanently delete your stored details at any time by emailing contact@nexbeck.com.

Who we are

We are Nexbeck, a specialised web design service dedicated to building high-converting websites for Australian electricians and trade contractors. We operate from Pakistan, delivering websites remotely to businesses throughout Australia.

Throughout this policy, references to "we", "us", or "our" mean Nexbeck. If you have questions regarding this policy or how your details are managed, you can contact us directly at contact@nexbeck.com.

What we collect

We only collect information that is directly necessary to communicate with you, evaluate your website needs, and deliver our design and development work. This includes:

  • Contact details: Your name, business name, email address, phone number, and physical or service area location.
  • Trade and project details: Electrical contractor licence numbers, trade specialities, preferred domain names, current website address (if any), and design preferences.
  • Website content and assets: Project photos, customer reviews, team bios, logos, and business copy provided for publication on your website.
  • Technical interaction data: Basic browser metadata, general location (country/city level), and page interaction metrics collected via aggregate analytics.

How we collect it

We collect information through transparent channels:

  • Direct submissions: When you complete our contact or quote request forms on nexbeck.com.
  • Direct correspondence: When you email, message, or speak with us to discuss your website project.
  • Client onboarding briefs: When you provide trade details, text, and photos for your website build.
  • Automated site analytics: When you browse nexbeck.com, standard analytics tools record aggregate technical data.

Why we use it

We use your information exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To reply to your questions and discuss website package options.
  • To conduct free website audits and provide honest recommendations.
  • To prepare project proposals, quotes, and project timelines.
  • To build, test, and launch your electrical website according to your brief.
  • To send operational updates, milestone notices, and invoices.
  • To maintain the security and reliable operation of our website.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to anyone.

To run our operations, host our website, and communicate with you, we rely on established service providers who process data strictly under our direction:

Service Provider Purpose Data Handled
Web3Forms Contact form processing and message delivery Name, email, phone, trade service, and message content
Zoho Mail Business email hosting and correspondence Email exchanges, project briefs, and quotes
Cloudflare DNS routing, CDN caching, and website security IP addresses, server logs, and security telemetry
Google Analytics (GA4) & Search Console Aggregate website performance analysis Anonymised usage patterns and search query trends

We do not permit these providers to use your information for their own independent marketing purposes.

Overseas handling

Nexbeck is based in and operates from Pakistan, while delivering website design services to clients located in Australia.

When you contact us or engage our services, your information is received and processed by our team in Pakistan. Additionally, the infrastructure providers listed above (such as Cloudflare, Zoho Mail, Web3Forms, and Google) maintain secure server networks across multiple countries, including the United States, Europe, and Australia. We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to ensure your data remains protected regardless of where it is processed.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses basic functional cookies and aggregate analytics tools:

  • Essential functionality: Temporary session cookies that help load pages cleanly and remember display preferences.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Collects anonymised information such as pages visited, device types, screen widths, and time spent on page. This helps us understand what content is helpful and where we can improve site performance.
  • Google Search Console: Provides aggregate visibility on how nexbeck.com appears in organic search results.

We do not use invasive third-party behavioural ad retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or TikTok tracking pixels) on nexbeck.com. You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time without preventing you from viewing our website content.

How long we keep it

We believe in keeping data only for as long as it serves a clear purpose:

  • General enquiries: Information submitted through our contact form is retained in our form records for 30 days. After 30 days, enquiry records that did not progress to an active project are cleared.
  • Active clients: If you engage us for a website project, we retain project correspondence, briefs, invoices, and completed website source files for the duration of our relationship and a reasonable archive period to assist you with future updates or support requests.
  • Tax and financial records: Payment receipts and invoices are retained for the standard accounting periods required by tax regulations.

Keeping it safe

We take practical, sensible steps to protect the information you share with us:

  • All website traffic to nexbeck.com is encrypted using HTTPS and TLS certificates managed through Cloudflare.
  • Email correspondence is handled through secure, password-protected Zoho Mail business accounts with multi-factor authentication enabled.
  • Administrative access to hosting dashboards, code repositories, and domain accounts is restricted to authorized team members.
  • We do not store credit card or debit card numbers on our servers.

Your choices

You have full control over your personal information. At any time, you may request to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal details we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to update or correct inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your contact details and message history from our active records.

To make any of these requests, simply email us at contact@nexbeck.com. We will respond promptly within 5 business days and without charging any fees.

Marketing emails

We do not send unsolicited marketing blasts, newsletter spam, or automated cold outreach to people who submit an enquiry. We communicate with you strictly regarding your specific question, quote, or project.

If we ever introduce an optional email newsletter with tips for trade websites, we will only send it to individuals who have given clear, affirmative consent, and every email will contain a single-click unsubscribe link.

Complaints

If you have a concern or complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please let us know so we can resolve it directly. Send an email to contact@nexbeck.com with the subject line "Privacy Concern".

We will review your message, investigate the issue, and provide a clear written response within 5 business days detailing the steps taken to address your concern.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational tools, service offerings, or website functionality. When we make updates, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we safeguard your details.

Questions about this policy?

If anything in this privacy policy is unclear or if you want to request data deletion, contact our team directly. We are happy to help.
